## `01 / THE IDEA`

AI FPS Booster is a Windows gaming optimization project built around one simple goal: **make your games feel smoother without deliberately lowering graphics quality**.

Instead of treating every PC the same, the goal is to focus on factors that can affect real-world performance: unnecessary background activity, system configuration, stability, and frame-time consistency. The aim is a more responsive gaming session while keeping the resolution, textures, shadows, and visual details you chose.

> **Performance first. Visual compromise not required.**

No two systems are identical. Your CPU, GPU, RAM, drivers, temperatures, game version, and settings all influence the result. AI FPS Booster does not promise the same FPS increase on every computer.

## `05 / NO-QUALITY-LOSS PHILOSOPHY`

The purpose is to optimize around your graphics settings — not to secretly trade image quality for a higher number.

- Keep your preferred resolution.
- Keep your preferred texture quality and visual details.
- Avoid unnecessary visual downgrades as a performance shortcut.
- Measure the results in the same game scene before and after optimization.

Actual behavior depends on the included features and your PC configuration. Some games are GPU-limited, some are CPU-limited, and others may be limited by temperatures, memory, or background processes. No utility can remove every bottleneck on every system.

## `06 / GET THE MOST OUT OF IT`

For a fair comparison, use the same game, map or scene, resolution, graphics settings, and approximate test duration before and after running the booster. Check both average FPS and frame-time consistency, and monitor CPU/GPU temperatures.

If performance becomes unstable, restore any settings you changed and stop using the relevant tweak. Keep Windows, game files, and graphics drivers up to date using official sources.
