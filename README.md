AI FPS Booster follows a simple philosophy: optimize performance without deliberately lowering your visual settings.

Keep your preferred resolution.
Keep your texture quality.
Keep your shadows and visual details.
Avoid unnecessary visual downgrades as a shortcut to higher FPS.
Compare performance before and after optimization.

No fake promise that every bottleneck can be removed. Some games are limited by the GPU, others by the CPU, memory, temperatures, or the game engine itself.

The goal is to identify opportunities for better performance while preserving the experience you chose.

07 / 📊 GET THE MOST OUT OF YOUR PC

Want to know whether the optimization actually helped?

Record your FPS in a repeatable game scene before optimization.
Keep your resolution and graphics settings unchanged.
Test the same scene again after optimization.
Compare average FPS and frame-time consistency.
Monitor CPU/GPU temperatures and system stability.

For a meaningful comparison, keep the game version, scene, graphics settings, and approximate test duration consistent.

If a tweak causes instability, restore the affected settings and stop using that tweak. Keep Windows, game files, and graphics drivers updated through official sources.
